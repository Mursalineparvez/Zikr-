## 📱 App Store / Google Play Store Listing Copy

### 🔹 App Title
**Zikr+ : Quran, Hadith, Tasbeeh & Islamic History**

### 🔹 Short Description (80 characters max)
Your complete Islamic companion: Digital Tasbeeh, 17 Hadith Books, Quran & History Atlas.

### 🔹 Full Description (English)
Transform your daily spiritual journey with **Zikr+**, the ultimate, feature-rich Islamic companion designed for modern Muslims. Featuring a stunning dark/day Emerald UI, haptic feedback, and offline-first capabilities, Zikr+ brings together all essential Islamic knowledge and daily worship tools in one seamless app.

#### ✨ Key Features:
1. **Interactive Digital Tasbeeh & Zikr Suite**
   - Glowing circular center counter with haptic feedback and audio clicks.
   - Live daily session stats and lifetime grand total counters.
   - Preloaded with essential Tasbeehat (SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, Darood, etc.) and custom zikr creator.

2. **Al-Quran al-Kareem**
   - Full 114 Surahs with pristine Arabic text and Bengali/English translations.
   - Audio recitation options (Mishary Rashid Alafasy and more).
   - Topic-wise Ayah indexing, bookmarking, and instant search.

3. **Comprehensive Hadith Library (17 Canonical Books)**
   - Over 53,000+ authentic hadiths across 17 complete books including *Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan an-Nasa\'i, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah, Riyad as-Salihin, Mishkat al-Masabih*, and more.
   - Advanced search, Arabic snippets, and scholarly references.

4. **Nobi 24 Hours (Sunnah Guideline)**
   - Prophet Muhammad ﷺ's daily 24-hour routine categorized into 14 timeline slots.
   - 12 comprehensive book chapters and daily Masnun Duas to live your day in accordance with the Sunnah.

5. **Seerat-un-Nabi & Interactive History Atlas**
   - Complete year-by-year chronological life journey of the Prophet ﷺ from Year 1 to 63.
   - Interactive History Map spanning 570 CE to 2026 CE with major milestones, routes, and events.
   - Comprehensive documentation of major Islamic Battles (Badr, Uhud, Khandaq, Yarmuk, Hattin, Ayn Jalut, Constantinople, etc.).

6. **Hayatus Sahaba (266+ Prominent Companions)**
   - Ranked biographies of 266+ noble Sahaba (RA), their sacrifices, emotional accounts, and verified Hadith citations.

7. **Daily Amal, Salat & Habit Tracker**
   - Track 5 daily prayers, Quran reading progress, and daily zikr goals with visual completion rings.

8. **Authentic Duas & Asma-ul-Husna**
   - Categorized Masnun Duas with audio support and 99 Beautiful Names of Allah (Asma-ul-Husna) with meanings and virtues.

9. **Hajj & Umrah Guide**
   - 3D animated Tawaf & Sa'i simulator, step-by-step preparation checklists, Miqat guides, and 5-day ritual itineraries.

10. **Saved Library & Cloud Sync**
    - Store bookmarks, highlights, and personal notes securely with optional Cloud sync.
